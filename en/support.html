NAGIRO Support

Support page for the camera app "NAGIRO".

Contact us

Please send questions, bug reports, and requests to the following email address.

mirairecipeapp@gmail.com

If you write the email from Settings → Contact & feedback in the App, the App and iOS versions are included in the body. If you contact us another way, including the following helps us investigate faster.

  • Your iOS version
  • Your NAGIRO version
  • What happened, and the steps you took

It may take some time to reply. Please do not include card numbers or passwords in your email.

When the free trial ends

For 30 days from the day you first obtain the App from the App Store, you can use every feature free of charge. When the trial ends, you return to the Free plan automatically and are never charged automatically. On the Free plan you can still take photos normally, use manual settings, lock settings, and view and edit favorites.

Canceling the Premium plan

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions to cancel. After canceling, the Premium plan remains available until the end of the current period. Purchases and refunds are handled under Apple's rules.

Switching to a new iPhone

You can carry over the Premium plan with "Restore Purchases" on the App's Plan screen. Favorites and photo records can be restored from your iPhone backup (including iCloud Backup). Voice history is not included in backups, so it is not carried over.

Deleting your data

From Settings → Data & privacy in the App, you can delete saved data by type, or all at once. Deleting all data does not reset your plan or AI shooting assist usage. Photos you took stay in your photo library, so delete them in the Photos app.

Camera, microphone, and location permissions

You can change permissions in the iPhone Settings app → NAGIRO. The microphone and location are used only when you use the features that need them.
