NAGIRO Privacy Policy

Established November 24, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the camera app "NAGIRO" (the "App") handles user information. This English translation is provided for convenience. If it differs from the Japanese version, the Japanese version prevails.

Key points

  • Camera analysis, speech recognition, and text interpretation all happen on your iPhone.
  • Photos, video, audio, and location are never sent to the operator or any external server.
  • The App has no advertising or usage analytics. The operator does not collect or sell users' personal information.

1. Camera and image analysis

The App uses the camera to take photos and to show suggestions for settings and composition based on the camera view. Analysis (brightness, composition, level, blur, color, and more) happens on the device, and the camera view is neither saved nor sent.

2. Photo library

The App adds photos you take to your photo library. It also reads the photo library to show and compare photos taken with the App on the Photo history screen. Photos are only displayed on the device and are never sent off it.

3. Location

The App uses your location only while the camera screen is open, and only when the setting to add the shooting location to photos is on. This setting is off by default. The location is only written into the photo; it is not stored in the App and not sent anywhere.

If you share a photo that contains location information, the people you share it with may learn where it was taken.

4. Microphone and speech recognition

The App uses the microphone only when you enter your shooting goal or shooting settings by voice. Audio is converted to text by on-device speech recognition only; it is never recorded, saved, or sent.

The language data used for speech recognition is downloaded from Apple by iOS only when you choose to download it. No audio is sent at that time.

When you change settings by voice, the recognized text and the settings it changed are kept on the device as "voice history" (the latest 20 entries, for 7 days). Voice history is not included in iPhone backups. You can delete it at any time from the history screen or the Data & privacy screen.

5. Text interpretation with Apple Intelligence

When you enter your shooting goal as text, devices that support Apple Intelligence interpret it with the on-device model. Cloud models are not used. The text you enter and the interpretation are not saved. On devices without support, the text is interpreted with simple rules on the device.

6. Data stored on the device

The App stores the following data on your device. The operator never receives this data.

  • Favorite settings, current settings, and settings history
  • Photo records (photo identifiers, and the settings, goal, and analysis summary at the time of shooting)
  • Personal style statistics (only when this feature is turned on; it is off by default)
  • Voice history (section 4)
  • Records such as usage dates used to determine the Free Trial and AI shooting assist usage (stored in the device keychain)

Except for voice history, this data is included in iPhone backups (including iCloud Backup) so it can be carried over to a new iPhone. Backups are stored in your own account on Apple's services, and the operator cannot view them.

You can delete data by type, or all at once, from the App's Data & privacy screen. However, the records used to determine the Free Trial and AI shooting assist usage remain even after deleting all data or deleting the App (so that the Free Trial and usage counts cannot be reset).

7. Notifications

The App uses notifications to let you know the day before your Free Trial ends. Notification permission is requested only when you turn this reminder on.

8. In-app purchases

Purchases of the Premium plan and purchase restores are processed by Apple through the App Store. The operator never receives payment information such as credit card numbers.

9. Network communication

The App does not communicate with the operator's servers or with advertising or analytics services. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Communication happens only for in-app purchases (section 8) and when iOS downloads speech recognition language data (section 4), and both are with Apple.

10. When you contact us

From the App's Contact & feedback screen, you send an email to the operator from your own mail app. The App never sends email itself. For troubleshooting, the email body includes the following information, and you can review the full text on screen before sending.

  • App version and build number, iOS version, language, and plan status (including Free Trial days remaining)

The device model, device name or identifiers, setting values, photo records, and photos are not included. Your email address and the content of your inquiry are used only to respond to you.

11. Changes to this policy

The operator may change this privacy policy when necessary. Changes will be announced on this page.
