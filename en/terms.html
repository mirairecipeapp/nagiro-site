Article 2 (What the App does)

The App is a camera app for taking photos and managing shooting settings (exposure, focus, white balance, zoom, and more). It analyzes the camera view on the device and shows suggestions for settings and composition ("AI shooting assist").

Settings the User has locked are never changed automatically by AI suggestions, templates, voice control, or similar features. AI suggestions are only shown; they are applied only when the User acts, or within the items and ranges the User has allowed to adjust automatically.

Settings the device does not support are shown as unavailable on screen. Available settings and ranges vary by device and camera.

Article 3 (Free trial and Free plan)

For 30 days from the day the User first obtains the App from the App Store, all features can be used free of charge (the "Free Trial"). The Free Trial involves no purchase and costs nothing. Deleting and reinstalling the App does not restart the Free Trial.

When the Free Trial ends, the User returns to the Free plan automatically. The User is never switched to a paid plan or charged automatically.

On the Free plan, some features such as AI shooting assist have limits on how many times or how many items can be used. The limits are shown on the App's Plan screen. Normal shooting, manual settings, locking settings, and viewing and editing favorites are not limited on the Free plan.

Article 4 (Premium plan)

The Premium plan is an auto-renewable subscription purchased through the App Store. Monthly and yearly options are available; the price and period are shown on the purchase screen.

The subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. To cancel, open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions. After canceling, the Premium plan remains available until the end of the current period.

Purchases and refunds are handled under Apple's rules. Canceling partway through a period does not refund the remaining period. When switching to a new iPhone, use "Restore Purchases" on the App's Plan screen to carry over a purchase.

Article 5 (Data storage and backups)

Data such as favorites, photo records, and settings history is stored on the User's device. The operator never receives this data.

This data is included in iPhone backups (including iCloud Backup), except for voice history, which is not included. Deleting the App also deletes the data on the device (except the records used to determine the Free Trial and AI shooting assist usage).

Photos taken are saved to the User's photo library. The User is responsible for making backups in case of data loss.

When the setting to add the shooting location to photos is on, location information is written into the photos. Be aware of this when sharing photos.
